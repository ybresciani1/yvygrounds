Garden Society Dash

The garden beds need help! Tap a tool in the shed (🌱 seeds, 💧 water, 🌿 weeding, 🪱 compost), then tap the bed that's asking for it. Tap a ripe bed to pick the harvest 🧺 and bring it to the farm stand. You can hold two things at once, and you can line up several taps. Same job in a row = combo! Keys: 1–4 tools, 5 farm stand, Q W E / A S D beds, X empties your hands. P pauses, M mutes.
