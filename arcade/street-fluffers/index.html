Street Fluffers

Press Start, pick a critter, then fight your way up the ladder! ← → or A D walk, ↑ or W jumps. J baps (or pecks), K kicks. L and I are your two special moves from your trading card (or go ↓ → + J / K, like the old days). Hold away from your opponent to block. On a phone, use the buttons on screen. P pauses, M mutes.
